The Midnight Chronicle

A journalism game about truth, speed and people

You are the reporter at a small village news site. The stories keep coming. Every headline you publish reaches real readers, and real people.

  • Information arrives on your desk in news cycles: police statements, emails, calls, posts online.
  • First judge how reliable each source is. Then write an article, check it first, or don't publish it.
  • The AI editor gives advice on your headline. Readers react to exactly what you write.
  • Watch your readers, public trust and story interest. If readers lose interest, your editor moves you to the next story.
  • See how much media pressure your reporting puts on the people in the story.
